# 🚀 Changelog — Speed Math (Last 2 Weeks)

## 🆕 New Game Modes (Arcade)
We have introduced three brand-new arcade experiences to diversify your mental training:
*   **🏎️ Math Kart**: A high-octane racing game where you steer your kart through gates containing the correct math answers.
*   **🛸 Math Invaders**: An action-packed mode where you must shoot down falling math expressions before they reach your base.
*   **🧱 Math Tetris**: A strategic puzzle game where you select falling blocks to reach a target sum and clear the board.

## 📊 Ranking & UI Enhancements
*   **Dynamic Ranking Display**: Overhauled the global leaderboard and league progress screens for better clarity and real-time updates.
*   **Contrast Optimization**: Enhanced the visual contrast for game cards and text in both **Light Mode** and **Dark Mode**.
*   **Theme Integration**: Game elements now dynamically adapt their colors based on the system theme to ensure maximum legibility.

## 🐞 Bug Fixes & Stability
*   **Fixed Lifecycle Crashes**: Resolved a critical `IllegalStateException` that occurred when games tried to access resources after a fragment was detached.
*   **Animation Synchronization**: Fixed a synchronization issue in *Math Kart* where waves would occasionally stall or move too slowly.
*   **Performance Tweak**: Optimized the spawn logic for arcade games to ensure smooth 60FPS performance on most devices.
*   **Memory Management**: Improved cleanup of background tasks and handlers to prevent memory leaks during extended play sessions.
