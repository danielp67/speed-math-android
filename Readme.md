# 🧠⚡ Speed Math — Think Fast, Solve Faster

**Speed Math** is a fast-paced mental math game designed to improve your calculation speed, focus, and competitive spirit.  
Play **solo**, challenge your friends **locally**, or face **real opponents online** in intense math battles.

## 🎯 Project Objective
The primary goal of Speed Math is to transform traditional mental arithmetic into an engaging, gamified experience. By combining high-speed gameplay with mathematical precision, the app helps users of all ages sharpen their cognitive abilities, reduce calculation latency, and build confidence in their mathematical skills through repetitive and varied challenges.

## 🌟 Intent & Philosophy
Speed Math is built with an "Education First" philosophy.
- **Fair & Ethical**: No hidden algorithms or pay-to-win mechanics.
- **Distraction-Free**: 100% Ad-free to maintain focus during training.
- **Inclusive**: Designed for everyone—from students looking to improve their grades to adults wanting to keep their brains sharp.
- **Privacy Centric**: Minimal data collection, strictly for ranking and matchmaking.

## 🎮 Game Modes

### 🧩 Solo & Training
*   **Arcade Mode**: Endless math challenges with dynamic difficulty scaling.
*   **League Mode**: Progress through structured levels and conquer the global leaderboard.
*   **Daily Challenges**: Special unique game modes that rotate daily to keep your training fresh.

### 🕹️ NEW Arcade Games
*   **🏎️ Math Kart**: Navigate a 4-lane track at high speed. You must steer your kart through the gate containing the correct answer to the math expression displayed on your dashboard. Speed increases as you progress!
*   **🛸 Math Invaders**: Protect your base! Shoot down falling mathematical expressions by selecting the one that matches the target result.
*   **🧱 Math Tetris**: A strategic numerical puzzle. Select falling blocks to sum up to a target number. Clear lines and manage the board before it reaches the top.

### 🧠 Memory Modes
*   **Memory**: Classic tile-matching game where operations must be paired with their correct results.
*   **Memory Duo**: A local 2-player battle on a shared board to see who has the best memory and fastest brain.

### ⚔️ Battle Modes
*   **Solo Battle**: Face off against a smart AI with adjustable difficulty levels.
*   **Dual Battle (Local)**: Challenge a friend on the same device in a split-screen math duel.
*   **Online QCM Battle**: Real-time multiplayer matchmaking. Compete against players worldwide to climb the Elo-based global ranking.

## 📊 Features
*   **Dynamic Difficulty**: The app adapts to your skill level in real-time.
*   **Global Ranking**: Track your progress and see where you stand against the world's best.
*   **Theme Support**: Full support for **Dark Mode** and **Light Mode**, with optimized contrast for all game elements.
*   **Smooth Performance**: High-frame-rate animations optimized for a wide range of Android devices.
*   **Haptic Feedback**: Tactile responses for correct and wrong answers to improve immersion.
